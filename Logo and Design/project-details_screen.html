Village Well Restoration

About the Project

This initiative aims to restore the historic central well in the village square. Over decades, structural cracks and sediment buildup have reduced water quality. Our goal is to reinforce the walls, install a sustainable solar-powered filtration system, and beautify the surrounding catchment area to serve as a reliable source of potable water for over 200 households.
